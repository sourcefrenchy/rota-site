Support

A person reads these.

Write to support@rota.app. ROTA is a small independent app, there is no ticket system and no bot, and replies usually arrive within two or three business days.

What to include

Your device and iOS version, the ROTA version from Settings → About, what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Please do not send photographs of your collection, serial numbers or screenshots containing them — we do not want them and we have nothing to store them in.

Requirements and compatibility

  • iOS 26 or later, on iPhone or iPad. One purchase covers both.
  • An iCloud account is optional. Without one, ROTA works fully on a single device.
  • No network connection is required for the daily suggestion, wear logging, the Box, Admire or the PDF export. Weather and the Journal need one.
  • English at launch. More languages are planned.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple Account you bought with, open ROTA, and tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings → Subscription. That re-checks your entitlements with Apple. If the paywall still appears after a restore, check that you are signed in to the purchasing Apple Account in Settings → your name → Media & Purchases — a different account is by far the most common cause.

How do I cancel, or change plan?

In Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Apple does not let developers cancel a subscription on a customer's behalf, so we cannot do it for you. Pro remains active until the end of the period you have paid for.

I cancelled. What happens to my watches?

Nothing. Every watch you have added stays visible and editable forever, whatever the count — see section 4 of the terms. The free tier's five-watch limit blocks only the adding of a sixth watch. Pro features go back to their free behaviour; your content is untouched.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no visibility of your payment and cannot issue one.

I bought Lifetime. Does my family get it?

Yes — the lifetime purchase has Family Sharing enabled. Members of your Family Sharing group can install ROTA and use Pro. Each person's collection is their own, in their own iCloud; Family Sharing shares the purchase, not the data.

iCloud sync

My iPad shows my watches but the photographs are missing.

They are still arriving. A collection of fifty watches with photographs is hundreds of megabytes, and iCloud delivers it over minutes or hours depending on your connection. ROTA shows every watch immediately as a typographic Specimen Card and fills the photographs in as they land. Settings → iCloud has a real progress row — "Photographs are still arriving from iCloud — 128 of 240" — so you can see it working. Keep the device on Wi-Fi and awake, and it will finish.

An edit on one device has not appeared on the other.

Check, in order: both devices signed in to the same Apple Account; iCloud Drive enabled in Settings → your name → iCloud; ROTA switched on in that same iCloud list; enough iCloud storage remaining; and the sync status row in ROTA's Settings, which will say Paused, on cellular or Signed out or show an error if one of these is the cause. Sync is not instantaneous — give it a minute with the app in the foreground.

I edited the same watch on two devices while offline. Did I lose something?

You should not have. Wear entries are an append-only log, so two devices logging the same day both survive, and the Box and Vitrine orderings merge rather than collide. If you do find something lost, duplicated or interleaved after a reconnection, that is a bug we want to hear about in detail: which two devices, which screen, what each one showed before and after.

Can I use ROTA without iCloud?

Yes. Signed out, everything works on that device alone. Nothing is hidden and no feature is withheld — you simply have one device's copy, and you should keep a device backup.

Weather

The weather glyph is missing and the suggestion ignores the forecast.

ROTA needs location permission to fetch weather. Check Settings → ROTA → Location is set to While Using the App, and check the weather assist is on in ROTA's own Assists settings. ROTA asks iOS for reduced accuracy, so precise location is neither needed nor requested.

If you decline, or you are offline, nothing breaks: the rotation engine renormalises without the weather axis and the glyph is simply absent.

Is the forecast accurate?

It is Apple Weather's, and it is as accurate as Apple Weather is. ROTA does not have its own forecast and cannot correct one.

Notifications

The morning notification did not arrive.

Check Settings → ROTA → Notifications is allowed, then check your suggestion time in ROTA's Settings, and check that a Focus or Sleep schedule is not filtering it. Notifications are scheduled locally, in advance — ROTA operates no push server — so a notification for tomorrow is written when you open the app today. Open the app once a day and it will always have one queued.

Some Care reminders never fire.

iOS allows any single app 64 pending notifications in total. ROTA schedules the twenty soonest Care reminders and re-rolls that window every time you open the app, so the rest are not lost — they are simply not scheduled yet. The Care room always shows the complete list, computed live.

The Journal

A feed is empty or has stopped updating.

Publishers change and retire their feed URLs without notice. A dead feed is shown as inactive rather than as an error, and switching it off and on again will retry. If a feed stays dead, tell us which one and we will fix the URL in the next release.

Why does ROTA only show the headline?

Deliberately. ROTA shows the headline, the source, the date and a link, and nothing else — no article text, no excerpt, no summary, no publisher imagery and no reader mode. We are a pointer to the publisher, never a substitute for them. Tapping a headline opens their own page, with their own layout and their own advertising.

Can I add my own feed?

Not in the first release. Tell us which one you want and it may make the registry.

The reference catalog

My watch is not in the catalog.

Add it manually — every field is editable and nothing about the app depends on a catalog match. The catalog is a typing shortcut, not a gate. Send us the brand, model and reference and we will consider it for a later release.

A catalog value is wrong.

Open the watch page and tap Report a correction. That opens your mail app with the catalog row and the app version filled in, and nothing else — no photographs, no collection, no identifiers. You see the message before it sends, and you can edit it. Corrections go to corrections@rota.app.

Prefilled fields are shown at reduced opacity with a gold underline until you confirm them, because catalog data is compiled from public sources and does contain errors. A wrong value degrades a suggestion; it never breaks one.

Export, backup and leaving

How do I get my collection out?

The PDF lookbook, from Settings → Export or from any watch page, renders on the device and is yours. Prices are excluded unless you switch them on at export time. Serial numbers are never included in a shared document.

Do you have a backup of my collection?

No, and that is the point — we never receive it. It also means we cannot restore it for you. Keep an iCloud or Finder device backup, keep your iCloud account in good standing, and export a lookbook occasionally for anything you would hate to retype.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app to remove the on-device store, then remove the iCloud copy in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → ROTA. There is nothing on our side to delete, and no request to file.

Accessibility

ROTA is built for VoiceOver end to end — adding a watch, logging a wear and exporting a lookbook are all completable without sight. Every screen reflows at Dynamic Type sizes up to AX3: score bars stack vertically, cards grow, nothing truncates. Reduce Motion replaces every animation with a short fade. Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: every state that has a colour also has a label.

If something is not usable for you, that is a bug and it gets priority. Tell us what you use and what happened.

Bugs and feature requests

Both to support@rota.app. For a bug, the most useful thing you can send is a reliable way to reproduce it. For a feature, tell us the problem you have rather than the solution you imagine — we will often find a better one.

Security and responsible disclosure

If you have pointed a proxy at ROTA and found a connection we do not name on the privacy page, that is exactly the finding we want. Write to privacy@rota.app with what you saw and how you saw it. We will confirm, fix and credit you if you want the credit.
