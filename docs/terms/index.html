ROTA has a free tier that does not expire. It holds up to five watches, plus unlimited straps, and includes the daily suggestion with weather, wear logging, the bars and heat-map, the Journal, the walnut box and Chronicle share cards.

We commit to one thing in particular, and it is a term of this agreement rather than a marketing line:

Every watch you have already added stays visible and editable, forever, at any count. The five-watch limit blocks only the adding of a sixth watch. It is a ceiling on growth, never a retraction of what exists.

Because there is nothing to expire, this section is mostly moot — but for the avoidance of doubt: nothing you created is ever hidden, greyed, watermarked, locked or made read-only. Pro features revert to their free-tier behaviour; your content does not.
